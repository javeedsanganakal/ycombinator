# Marketing and Growth Projects

Use these projects to study acquisition, attribution, lifecycle communication, and conversion. A tool cannot substitute for a clear segment, promise, or evidence.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

| Project | Study it for | Inspect closely | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog) | Product-led growth, funnels, cohorts, surveys, replay, and experiments | Event model, activation definitions, retention views, feature flags | More behavioral data does not resolve an undefined customer or outcome |
| [Plausible](https://github.com/plausible/analytics) | Privacy-conscious website acquisition analytics | Traffic sources, campaigns, goals, funnels, data minimization | Website analytics alone cannot measure account-level B2B revenue |
| [Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami) | Lightweight web analytics and event tracking | Events, sessions, funnels, retention, teams, hosting model | Decide identity and account stitching before treating sessions as customers |
| [Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic) | Marketing automation and lifecycle journeys | Segments, campaigns, lead scoring, email events, consent | Automation scales both good and bad messaging; verify deliverability and privacy rules |
| [listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk) | Newsletter and mailing-list operations | Lists, segmentation, templates, campaigns, bounces, opt-outs | Self-hosting does not remove consent, sender-reputation, or anti-spam obligations |
| [Dub](https://github.com/dubinc/dub) | Links, attribution, conversion tracking, and partner programs | Link schema, conversion events, domains, commissions, fraud controls | Last-click attribution should not be treated as causal proof |
| [React Email](https://github.com/resend/react-email) | Component-based lifecycle and transactional email | Accessible components, rendering, previews, provider integration | Templates do not provide sending infrastructure, consent, or deliverability |

## Channel evidence

For every marketing channel, record audience fit, message, offer, distribution mechanism, cost, conversion definition, sales or activation handoff, retention quality, and stop/continue rule. Measure qualified and retained customers—not reach alone.
