# Product Management and UX/UI Projects

Use these projects to study discovery, planning, delivery, research, prototyping, components, accessibility, and design-to-code systems.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

## Product systems

| Project | Study it for | Inspect closely | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Plane](https://github.com/makeplane/plane) | Modern product planning and issue workflows | Cycles, modules, views, intake, workspaces, integrations | A polished roadmap system cannot prioritize without strategy and evidence |
| [OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject) | Project, portfolio, roadmap, time, and team workflows | Work packages, dependencies, roadmaps, roles, reporting | Its breadth may be unnecessary for a small product team |
| [Taiga](https://github.com/taigaio/taiga-back) | Agile project concepts and API design | Epics, stories, tasks, sprints, Kanban, permissions | Process should be adapted to team constraints rather than copied wholesale |
| [Formbricks](https://github.com/formbricks/formbricks) | Product research and contextual feedback | Targeting, triggers, question design, response analysis | Self-reported feedback should be triangulated with observed behavior |

## UX, UI, and design systems

| Project or standard | Study it for | Inspect closely | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Penpot](https://github.com/penpot/penpot) | Collaborative product design, prototypes, tokens, and design-to-code | Components, variants, layouts, tokens, inspect mode | Tooling cannot replace research or usability testing |
| [Storybook](https://github.com/storybookjs/storybook) | Isolated UI development and component documentation | Stories, controls, interaction tests, accessibility, visual tests | A component catalog is not automatically a coherent product experience |
| [Radix Primitives](https://github.com/radix-ui/primitives) | Low-level accessible interaction primitives | Keyboard behavior, focus, composition, states, ARIA | Styling and product-specific usability remain the team's responsibility |
| [shadcn/ui](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui) | Editable component patterns and application UI composition | Component source, accessibility foundations, theming, registry | Reusing the default visual style can make products undifferentiated |
| [Headless UI](https://github.com/tailwindlabs/headlessui) | Unstyled accessible UI behavior | State, keyboard interactions, transitions, framework integration | Coverage is intentionally narrower than a complete design system |
| [Design Tokens Community Group](https://github.com/design-tokens/community-group) | Interoperable design-token standards | Format modules, naming, types, resolver behavior | Track specification maturity and tool support before standardizing |
| [GOV.UK Design System](https://github.com/alphagov/govuk-design-system) | Evidence-driven accessible service patterns | Content, errors, forms, navigation, accessibility research | Government-service patterns require adaptation to product context |
| [18F Methods](https://github.com/18F/methods) | Practical user-centered research and design methods | Method selection, preparation, synthesis, and outputs | Methods create evidence only when recruiting and facilitation are sound |
