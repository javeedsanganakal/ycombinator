## How to use an entry

1. Start with a concrete decision or bottleneck.
2. Read the project's documentation, data model, workflows, issues, and changelog—not only its homepage.
3. Extract the smallest useful pattern: an object model, funnel event, screen flow, billing state, operating cadence, or control.
4. Compare at least two projects when the decision is important.
5. Record why the pattern fits the product's users, stage, economics, and constraints.
6. Build or adopt only what is necessary for the current learning goal.

Use the [startup templates](../templates/) to turn findings into a product brief, research plan, positioning, pipeline, pricing test, experiment, launch review, or weekly operating cadence.

## Guardrails

- Inclusion is not an endorsement and popularity is not product fit.
- Verify current licensing, security policy, releases, hosting terms, and data practices at the source before adoption.
- Do not copy a competitor's UX, pricing, or claims without understanding the customer and economic context.
- Prefer direct customer evidence over an attractive open-source implementation.
- Link to third-party work rather than copying it unless its license and provenance permit redistribution.
