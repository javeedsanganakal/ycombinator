# Finance, Fundraising, and Legal Sources

Use primary sources to identify decisions, records, and professional-review points. This material is educational and is not legal, tax, accounting, investment, or employment advice.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

## Finance and company records

| Source | Use it for | Startup application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [IRS: Starting a Business](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/starting-a-business) | US federal tax basics and recordkeeping entry points | Confirm structure, EIN, tax responsibilities, accounting period, records, and federal resources |
| [SBA: Calculate Startup Costs](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide/plan-your-business/calculate-your-startup-costs) | Separating one-time and recurring startup expenses | Build the initial cash requirement and break-even assumptions |
| [Open Cap Table Format](https://github.com/Open-Cap-Table-Coalition/Open-Cap-Format-OCF) | Standardized equity transaction data | Keep issuances, stakeholders, vesting, conversions, exercises, and cancellations structurally auditable |

Maintain a monthly close with bank reconciliation, revenue and expense classification, accounts receivable/payable, payroll and contractor records, tax obligations, capitalization changes, cash balance, committed spend, runway scenarios, and material variances.

## Fundraising

| Source | Use it for | Startup application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Y Combinator SAFE documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents/) | Current YC post-money SAFE forms and user guide | Model ownership and dilution, choose the correct jurisdictional form, and obtain counsel |
| [SEC Resources for Small Businesses](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses) | US capital-raising pathways and regulatory entry points | Understand registered and exempt pathways before soliciting or accepting investment |
| [SEC Exempt Offerings](https://www.sec.gov/about/exempt-offerings) | Common US securities-registration exemptions | Review solicitation, investor, filing, disclosure, resale, and state-law implications with counsel |
| [NVCA Model Legal Documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) | Current model US venture-financing documents | Understand priced-round document relationships and market provisions with counsel |

Before fundraising, reconcile the cap table, model dilution across scenarios, define amount and milestone, document use of funds and runway, prepare claims with evidence, organize diligence, identify applicable offering rules, and create a consistent investor-update cadence.

## Data-room index

Organize incorporation and governance, capitalization, financing, material contracts, intellectual property assignments, employment and contractor records, financial statements, tax records, product/security/privacy documentation, customer and revenue evidence, litigation or claims, and regulatory matters. Restrict access and log disclosures according to sensitivity.
