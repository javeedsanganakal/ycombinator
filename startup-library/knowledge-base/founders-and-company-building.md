# Founders and Company-Building Projects

Use these public systems to study how companies document decisions, communicate, hire, operate equity data, and execute agreements. They are examples, not universal company policy or legal advice.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

| Project or public system | Study it for | Inspect closely | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [PostHog Handbook](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog.com/tree/master/contents/handbook) | Transparent startup operations across product, engineering, growth, people, and finance | Decision rights, goals, hiring, compensation, sales, growth, communication | Practices reflect PostHog's stage, culture, and open-source model |
| [GitLab Handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/) | Large-scale remote and asynchronous company documentation | Handbook structure, roles, workflows, communication, product process | Scale and process depth differ sharply from an early startup |
| [37signals Handbook](https://github.com/basecamp/handbook) | Concise policies, benefits, roles, and operating expectations | Policy clarity, onboarding, career expectations, work practices | Policies are jurisdiction- and company-specific |
| [Open Cap Table Format](https://github.com/Open-Cap-Table-Coalition/Open-Cap-Format-OCF) | A standard data model for capitalization-table events | Securities, stakeholders, transactions, vesting, validation, versioning | Use qualified legal and equity-administration guidance for actual records |
| [DocuSeal](https://github.com/docusealco/docuseal) | Document preparation, signing, identity, storage, and workflow | Templates, recipients, audit trail, signatures, APIs, storage | Electronic-signature validity and retention requirements vary by jurisdiction |
| [Documenso](https://github.com/documenso/documenso) | Open document-signing product and trust infrastructure | Envelopes, recipients, fields, certificates, auditability, self-hosting | Review signing standards, security, and legal enforceability for the use case |

## Founder extraction method

For each public handbook or system, capture the company context, problem solved, decision rule, owner, cadence, tradeoff, failure mode, and the earliest stage at which the practice becomes useful. Avoid importing policy text without adapting it.
