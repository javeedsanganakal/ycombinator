# Hiring and Operations Sources

Use these references and controls to build an early operating system. Employment, payroll, benefits, worker classification, leave, safety, and privacy requirements vary by jurisdiction; obtain qualified advice.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

## Hiring and employment

| Source | Use it for | Startup application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [EEOC Prohibited Employment Policies and Practices](https://www.eeoc.gov/prohibited-employment-policiespractices) | US federal anti-discrimination considerations across employment decisions | Review job requirements, sourcing, interviews, tests, pay, promotion, discipline, and termination |
| [EEOC Small Business Resources](https://www.eeoc.gov/employers/small-business) | Coverage, responsibilities, records, notices, and support | Identify which federal duties apply and where state/local review is also needed |
| [US Department of Labor: Worker Misclassification](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa/misclassification) | Employee-versus-contractor analysis under the FLSA | Evaluate the real working relationship rather than relying on contract labels |
| [USCIS Form I-9 Central](https://www.uscis.gov/i-9-central) | US employment eligibility verification | Establish authorized, timely, privacy-conscious verification and retention procedures |

## Role and hiring system

1. Write the business outcome and why the role is needed now.
2. Define 30/60/90-day outcomes, required capabilities, evidence, constraints, and compensation range.
3. Use structured sourcing, consistent questions, work samples tied to the role, and a written scorecard.
4. Separate interview evidence from intuition; document the hiring decision and conflicts.
5. Complete classification, agreement, IP/confidentiality, payroll, benefits, access, security, and onboarding requirements.
6. Review quality of hire, time to productivity, retention, fairness, and candidate experience.

## Company operating cadence

| Cadence | Minimum output |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Customer/revenue movement, product delivery, incidents, cash changes, decisions, owners, and blockers |
| Monthly | Financial close, runway scenarios, funnel and cohort review, security/privacy risks, hiring plan, and priority reset |
| Quarterly | Strategy assumptions, segment and positioning, roadmap, operating plan, risks, team design, and board/investor update |
| Event-driven | Incident response, material contract, financing, hiring/termination, access change, legal claim, and data-rights request |

## Minimum control register

Track bank and payment access, spending approval, contracts, cap-table changes, payroll, tax filings, employee/contractor lifecycle, production access, secrets, backups, dependency updates, incident response, privacy requests, vendor reviews, insurance, customer support escalation, and business continuity. Each control needs an owner, frequency or trigger, evidence, exception path, and last review.
