# Stanford and Y Combinator Startup Map

This guide connects official Stanford and Y Combinator resources, the repository's licensed lecture archive, and its searchable YC company data. Use it to answer a specific startup question—not as proof that a tactic will work for every company.

Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

## Stanford primary resources

| Resource | Best use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Stanford Technology Ventures Program: Start Here](https://stvp.stanford.edu/start) | Current courses, the Entrepreneurial Thought Leaders series, and Stanford entrepreneurship pathways | Some courses and programs require Stanford enrollment |
| [Stanford eCorner](https://ecorner.stanford.edu/) | Searchable talks, clips, articles, and educator resources from founders, operators, and researchers | Speaker experience is contextual evidence, not a universal rule |
| [Lean LaunchPad](https://leanlaunchpad.stanford.edu/) | Business-model hypotheses, customer discovery, weekly evidence, rapid iteration, and product-market-fit search | The published class pace is demanding; adapt effort to risk and access |
| [Stanford LaunchPad curriculum](https://www.launchpad.stanford.edu/curriculum) | Action-based venture building from problem through prototype, customers, and investors | The course is an accelerator and its participation model is not generally available |
| [Stanford LaunchPad resources](https://www.launchpad.stanford.edu/resources) | Founder hypotheses, customer/market tools, experiments, and product-market-fit work | Verify linked third-party tools independently |
| [Startup Garage](https://www.gsb.stanford.edu/experience/learning/entrepreneurship/courses/startup-garage) | Design thinking, needs discovery, prototyping, business models, and company creation | Course participation is for eligible Stanford students |
| [Stanford d.school Resources](https://dschool.stanford.edu/resources) | Human-centered research, framing, ideation, prototyping, and facilitation methods | Methods require sound recruiting and evidence interpretation |

## Y Combinator primary resources

| Resource | Best use | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [YC Library](https://www.ycombinator.com/library) | Searchable founder guidance across ideas, product, growth, sales, fundraising, hiring, and leadership | Publication date and startup context matter |
| [Startup School talks](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-videos) | A compact curriculum on ideas, cofounders, users, pricing, MVPs, customers, launching, and technical founders | Watching is not a substitute for customer action |
| [YC Company Directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) | Current official company profiles and directory filters | Directory inclusion and descriptions do not establish performance or comparability |
| [YC Launches](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches) | Study launch language, categories, target users, and product presentation | Launch copy is company-provided promotional material |
| [Work at a Startup](https://www.ycombinator.com/jobs) | Current startup roles, skills, locations, and compensation signals | Job listings change quickly and are not a complete labor-market sample |
| [SAFE documents](https://www.ycombinator.com/documents/) | Current YC post-money SAFE forms and user guide | Obtain counsel and use the correct jurisdictional form |
| [Template sales agreements](https://www.ycombinator.com/sales_agreement/) | Starting points for SaaS sales and business-associate agreements | Templates require deal-, product-, data-, and jurisdiction-specific legal review |
| [How to Sell](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-sell-by-tyler-bosmeny/) | Founder-led sales, pipeline, discovery, demos, closing, and early hiring | Apply to the customer economics and buying process |

## YC data in this repository

The repository currently contains 6,220 company records, 455 mirrored community-maintained `yc-oss` endpoint payloads, and the mirror index. It includes batch, industry, tag, status, region, stage, descriptions, official YC profile links, curated company views, and public open-source repository mappings where available.

Use the [YC dataset research guide](../../datasets/yc-research-guide.md) to search and filter the data. Treat it as a research index: verify time-sensitive company facts against the [official YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) and company primary sources before making decisions.

## Synthesis rule

For every Stanford or YC lesson, record the source date, speaker or author, company/stage context, claimed mechanism, evidence, counterexample, relevance to the current product, and the test that would validate it. Do not convert institutional reputation into product evidence.
