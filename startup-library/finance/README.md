# Finance

Runway, burn, budgeting, forecasts, cash management, accounting, taxes, unit economics, SaaS metrics, financial controls, and board reporting.

## Financial operating system

1. Separate personal and company funds; define approval and banking access.
2. Use consistent double-entry records and reconcile bank, processor, invoice, payroll, and expense balances monthly.
3. Track cash balance, restricted cash, receivables, payables, committed spend, taxes, payroll, debt, and capitalization events.
4. Maintain base, downside, and hiring/investment scenarios with explicit assumptions.
5. Review unit economics by product, plan, segment, and cohort where data permits.
6. Close the books, explain material variance, update runway, and assign corrective decisions every month.

## Core formulas

```text
Net burn = cash operating outflows - cash operating inflows
Runway months = available cash / expected monthly net burn
Gross margin = (revenue - direct cost to serve) / revenue
CAC = attributable acquisition and sales cost / new customers acquired
CAC payback months = CAC / monthly gross profit per new customer
Net revenue retention = (starting recurring revenue - contraction - churn + expansion) / starting recurring revenue
```

Define cash versus accrual treatment, attribution, direct costs, customer unit, cohort, and time window before using these formulas.
