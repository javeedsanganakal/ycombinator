# Fundraising

Bootstrapping, funding strategy, investor research, pitch decks, outreach, meetings, term sheets, dilution, due diligence, data rooms, and investor updates.

## Fundraising sequence

1. Decide whether financing is necessary for a specific milestone and whether customer revenue, debt, grants, or equity best matches the risk and timing.
2. Define the amount, minimum viable round, use of funds, milestone, runway, and downside plan.
3. Reconcile company records, capitalization, intellectual property, contracts, financials, product evidence, and material risks.
4. Build a concise narrative: problem, product, customer, insight, market, traction, business model, competition, team, and ask.
5. Research investors by stage, sector, geography, check size, conflicts, reputation, decision process, and portfolio fit.
6. Run a concentrated process with consistent information, tracked next steps, professional follow-up, and securities-law review.
7. Model dilution and control terms, complete diligence, close correctly, and communicate on a reliable cadence afterward.

Interest, introductions, verbal enthusiasm, and unsigned commitments are not cash. Track signed documents, conditions, funds received, and updated capitalization separately.
