# Founders

Startup ideas, problem selection, customer validation, cofounder relationships, founder-market fit, decision-making, resilience, ethics, and the transition from founder to company leader.

## Founder operating loop

1. **Observe reality.** Review customer behavior, commercial commitments, product quality, cash, and material risks.
2. **Name the constraint.** Choose the first weak and consequential transition rather than declaring every problem urgent.
3. **Make the decision.** Record assumptions, options, evidence, owner, reversibility, deadline, and review trigger.
4. **Run the smallest test.** Prefer actions that can disprove the belief or produce a customer commitment.
5. **Deliver reliably.** Keep promises to customers, teammates, investors, and partners; surface bad news early.
6. **Update the model.** Preserve decisions and evidence so the company learns instead of repeating debates.

## Founder scorecard

- Customer conversations grounded in recent behavior
- Product activation and repeat value
- Revenue quality and cash collection
- Burn, committed spend, and runway scenarios
- Delivery reliability and unresolved incidents
- Hiring quality, ownership, and team health
- Legal, security, privacy, accessibility, and reputational risks
- Three current priorities and explicit work stopped
