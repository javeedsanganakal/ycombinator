# Hiring and Culture

Workforce planning, early hires, sourcing, interviewing, compensation, onboarding, performance, management, organizational design, communication, and culture.

## Early hiring path

1. Prove the recurring business need and why founders or automation cannot own it.
2. Define outcomes, decision rights, constraints, evidence of capability, and a lawful compensation range.
3. Use observable criteria, structured interviews, role-relevant work samples, consistent scoring, and documented decisions.
4. Complete classification, eligibility, agreement, intellectual property, payroll, benefits, access, security, and equipment requirements.
5. Provide a 30/60/90-day plan with context, relationships, first delivery, feedback, and success evidence.
6. Review time to productivity, performance, retention, candidate experience, fairness, and whether the role still matches the constraint.

Culture is the repeated system of decisions, incentives, information flow, accountability, and behavior—not a list of aspirational adjectives.
