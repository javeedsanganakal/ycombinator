# Operations

Company cadence, goal setting, documentation, customer support, procurement, vendor management, internal tooling, risk, incident response, and repeatable processes.

## Operational design

Every recurring process should state its trigger, owner, inputs, steps, decision rights, evidence, expected time, escalation, exception path, output, and review date. Automate only after the workflow and failure handling are understood.

## Minimum registers

- Decisions and assumptions
- Metrics and definitions
- Customers, contracts, renewals, support escalations, and commitments
- Vendors, subprocessors, cost, access, renewal, risk, and exit plan
- Company and capitalization records
- Employees, contractors, payroll, benefits, access, and lifecycle events
- Security, privacy, incidents, vulnerabilities, backups, and recovery tests
- Financial close, taxes, cash commitments, budget, and approvals

Review critical access and cash weekly, close operations and finances monthly, reassess strategy and risks quarterly, and trigger incident/legal/privacy/customer escalation immediately when defined thresholds are crossed.
