# Legal

Company formation, founder equity, cap tables, intellectual property, employment, commercial contracts, privacy, compliance, and governance. Material here is educational and is not legal advice.

## Legal issue map

| Trigger | Review area |
| --- | --- |
| Forming or changing the company | Entity, jurisdiction, governance, tax, founder agreements, equity, vesting, elections, and records |
| Building the product | Founder/employee/contractor IP assignment, licenses, trademarks, data rights, accessibility, security, and regulated use |
| Hiring or contracting | Classification, eligibility, pay, equity, benefits, confidentiality, IP, policies, records, and termination |
| Selling | Terms, order form, scope, service level, privacy/DPA, security, warranty, liability, indemnity, payment, tax, and renewal |
| Marketing | Claims, endorsements, reviews, email, cookies/tracking, promotions, platform rules, and sector restrictions |
| Raising money | Securities exemptions, solicitation, disclosures, approvals, documents, filings, capitalization, and investor rights |
| Entering a market | Consumer, privacy, employment, tax, payments, export/sanctions, industry, and local requirements |

Maintain a legal register with entity deadlines, approvals, equity events, material contracts, licenses, IP, privacy processing, security commitments, insurance, disputes, filings, owners, renewal/expiry dates, and counsel decisions.

Browse the [founders and company-building knowledge base](../knowledge-base/founders-and-company-building.md) for cap-table data and document-workflow references. Verify legal decisions with qualified counsel in the applicable jurisdiction.
