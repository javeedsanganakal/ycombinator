# Case Studies

Structured startup analyses covering context, decisions, execution, outcomes, lessons, primary sources, and what remains uncertain.

## Case-study standard

```text
Company/product and period:
Stage, segment, market, and constraints:
Decision under study:
Alternatives available at the time:
Evidence available before the decision:
Action, sequence, owner, and investment:
Observed outcome and measurement window:
Confounders and missing evidence:
What is directly sourced versus inferred:
Transferable mechanism:
Conditions where the lesson should not be applied:
Primary sources and access dates:
```

Avoid survivor bias, outcome-only storytelling, present-day knowledge projected backward, and lessons based only on founder memory or promotional material. A useful case explains the mechanism and boundary conditions, not merely the result.
