## Interview guide

1. Tell me about the last time you did `[job]`.
2. What triggered it, and what was at stake?
3. Walk me through the process, tools, people, time, and handoffs.
4. Where did it break, slow down, create risk, or require rework?
5. What have you tried? What did it cost, and why did it remain insufficient?
6. Who decides whether to change the workflow? Who controls budget and implementation?
7. What deadline or event would make solving this urgent?
8. What evidence would you need before trying or buying an alternative?
9. What happened after the workflow—what outcome was measured?
10. May I observe the next occurrence or review a redacted artifact?

Avoid asking whether the person “likes” an idea or would hypothetically use it. Request recent examples, artifacts, behavior, spend, commitments, and consequences.
