## Product and evidence

- [ ] The intended user, job, promise, alternative, and capability boundaries are clear.
- [ ] The representative happy path and important failure paths pass.
- [ ] Unfamiliar users can reach first value without founder intervention, or concierge support is explicit.
- [ ] Claims, screenshots, demos, testimonials, and benchmarks are current and substantiated.
- [ ] Pricing, limits, taxes, billing, cancellation, refunds, and support expectations are understandable.

## Trust and operations

- [ ] Security, privacy, accessibility, data retention/deletion, and applicable compliance reviews are complete.
- [ ] Dependencies, secrets, access, backups, migrations, monitoring, alerts, incident response, and status communication are ready.
- [ ] Terms, privacy notice, licenses, IP ownership, commercial agreements, and platform policies are reviewed where applicable.
- [ ] Support channels, owner, coverage, escalation, known issues, and response expectations are documented.
- [ ] Capacity, vendor limits, unit costs, abuse controls, and failure budgets are tested.

## Measurement and rollout

- [ ] Eligibility, acquisition, first value, activation, payment, retention, and guardrail events are validated end to end.
- [ ] Dashboards use documented identities, denominators, cohorts, and time windows.
- [ ] Rollout stages, holdback or comparison, stop conditions, rollback steps, and customer communication are defined.
- [ ] The team knows which decision will be made at 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, and the retention window.
