## Message hierarchy

- Headline: audience + valuable outcome
- Subhead: how it works + meaningful differentiation
- Proof: customer behavior, quantified result, demonstration, or benchmark
- Primary call to action: the next customer commitment
- Objection handling: alternative, trust, implementation, price, and timing
- Capability boundary: what the product does not do

## Message variants

```text
One sentence:
For [specific audience] who [painful job/trigger], [product] is a [category]
that [measurable outcome]. Unlike [alternative], it [differentiated capability/proof].

Outbound opener:
I noticed [observable trigger]. How are you handling [specific workflow] today?

Demo opener:
You said [current workflow] causes [consequence]. I will show only the path from
[input] to [outcome], then we can decide whether a real evaluation makes sense.
```

## Validation

Test comprehension, relevance, credibility, differentiation, and action separately. Record segment, message, exposure, response, qualified action, activation, and retention; do not optimize clicks alone.
