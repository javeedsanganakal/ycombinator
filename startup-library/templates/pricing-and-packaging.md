## Economics

Define gross margin, acquisition cost, payback, retention, expansion, support load, infrastructure/AI cost, payment fees, refunds, taxes, annual-discount cash effect, and downside under heavy usage.

## Research tests

- Review the last real purchase and budget process, not abstract price preference.
- Present a concrete offer and request a paid pilot, deposit, checkout, or signed commitment.
- Test package comprehension and limit predictability.
- Analyze conversion, activation, retention, support, margin, and expansion by segment and cohort.
- Protect existing commitments and document migration, downgrade, cancellation, and refund behavior.
