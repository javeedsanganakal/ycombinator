## How to use this snapshot

1. Start with a question such as “Which recent companies sell infrastructure to AI-agent teams?”
2. Use `ycombinator filter --batch "Fall 2026"` or `ycombinator search "agent"` to widen or narrow the set.
3. Open the tracked JSON record and the official profile, then verify the company's site before using the result in a market, sales, or pricing decision.
4. Record the comparison question, source date, evidence, and uncertainty in the relevant startup template.

The snapshot is not a ranking, investment recommendation, or proof of company performance.
